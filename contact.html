Find us

Getting here

Our main clinic is at 427 Whitehorse Rd, Balwyn, and the 411 clinic is a short walk along the same road. There's free parking behind the clinic (behind Priceline Pharmacy), the 109 tram stops close by, and the practice has wheelchair access.

After hours

When we're closed

After hours, home visits for existing patients are available through 13SICK on 13 74 25.
