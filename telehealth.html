Telehealth

See your doctor from home

Book a phone or video consult with one of our GPs. It suits scripts, referrals, test results, mental health support and follow-up care, without leaving the house.

Book a telehealth consult Call reception
How it works

Four simple steps

Getting seen from home only takes a few steps.

Step 01

Book

Choose a telehealth appointment through online booking or by calling reception.

Step 02

We confirm

You'll get a confirmation with your appointment time and how your doctor will reach you.

Step 03

Consult from home

Speak with your GP by phone or video at your appointment time, wherever you are.

Step 04

Scripts and referrals

Prescriptions, referrals and requests are sent through, with follow-up arranged as needed.

What telehealth suits

What we can help with

Telehealth works well for many everyday health needs. If your doctor feels you'd be better seen in person, they'll let you know and help arrange a visit.

  • Repeat prescriptions and medication reviews
  • Referrals to specialists and allied health
  • Talking through test and pathology results
  • Mental health support and care plans
  • Follow-ups and management of ongoing conditions
  • Medical certificates and general advice

Good to know before you book

  • Telehealth is available to patients who have seen one of our doctors face to face in the last 12 months.
  • It's billed in line with our standard fee schedule.
  • Please check your phone number when you book, and be reachable from 5 minutes before your appointment.
  • Consults can run late, so please stay available for up to 30 minutes after your appointment time.
  • Your doctor will call twice. If we can't reach you, the appointment will need to be rescheduled.
  • Medicare doesn't allow telehealth consults while you're overseas.
  • In a medical emergency, call 000.
