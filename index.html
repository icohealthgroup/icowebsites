Our practice

We look after families here, often the same ones, year after year.

Balwyn Central Medical opened in 2015 and is a fully accredited general practice, part of ICO Health Group. Across our two clinics on Whitehorse Road you'll find GPs with a wide range of interests, registered nurses on site, and a pharmacy and pathology collection in the same building.

It means you can see your doctor, fill a script and have tests done in one visit, with a team that already knows your history.
