Questions

Fees FAQs

The full consultation fee, including the Medicare rebate, is charged at your appointment. The rebate is then processed back to your account afterwards. Please bring your Medicare card and any concession or DVA cards so we can process your rebate correctly.

The gap is your out-of-pocket fee: the part of the fee Medicare doesn't cover. On weekdays it is for non-concession patients. On weekends it is on Saturdays and on Sundays for all patients. Fees may vary with the length of your consultation, but the out-of-pocket amount stays the same for valid Medicare cardholders.

Telehealth appointments are billed in line with the fee schedule on this page.

The full fee is settled at your appointment. We accept all major debit and credit cards, including American Express, and there's no card surcharge. We generally don't accept cash.

For WorkCover and TAC, your first appointment is paid in full on the day, and you can then claim it back from the insurer or your employer. Once you have a claim number, bring it to your next visit and we'll bill the insurer directly. If you don't have your claim number yet, that visit is also paid on the day.

If you're covered by other insurance, such as overseas student or visitor cover, you pay the full invoice on the day and then claim it back from your insurer.
