General practice

Everyday GP care

Our GPs and registrars look after patients of every age, from routine check-ups and vaccinations to complex and ongoing conditions. With registered nurses always on site, many procedures and health checks can be done on the day.

Whether it's a health check, a script, a referral or something more involved, we keep your whole health picture in one place.

Skin checks

Skin cancer checks

A careful look at your skin, with follow-up in the same practice if anything needs it.

Skin cancer is common in Australia, so it's worth checking in with your GP about your skin. Our doctors do full-body skin examinations and spot checks, using dermoscopy to look closely at moles and lesions.

If something needs attention, a biopsy or excision can often be arranged on site, so you're looked after from the check through to treatment.

Bulk billing is not available for skin checks. You'll find the fees, and the doctors who do skin checks, in Find care.

Visiting specialist

Obstetrics and gynaecology

Dr Samina Kausar (FRANZCOG, FRCOG, FCPS) is a consultant obstetrician and gynaecologist who consults at Balwyn. She helps with periods and general gynaecology, menopause, contraception, abnormal cervical screening results and colposcopy, and pelvic floor concerns.

Dr Kausar performs Mirena (IUD) insertion as a day procedure here at the clinic, so there's no need to go to hospital. She speaks English, Urdu, Punjabi and Hindi.

You'll need a referral from your GP. Ask your doctor at your next visit, or call reception and we'll arrange it.

After hours

When the clinic is closed

We consult on weekends at 427 Whitehorse Rd until 5:30pm. Home visits for existing patients are available through 13SICK on 13 42 25, from 6pm on weekdays and from 10am on weekends and public holidays.

In an emergency, always call 000.

Under one roof

Pharmacy and pathology in the building

Priceline Pharmacy Balwyn

At 427 Whitehorse Rd, in the same building as the practice, so you can fill your script before you head home.

Dorevitch pathology collection

Walk in, with no appointment needed, Monday to Friday from 8:30am to 12pm. Dorevitch's own collectors take your samples, and your results come back to your GP for review and follow-up.
