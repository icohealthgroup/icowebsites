Our allied health team

Where a provider is on our HotDoc page you can book online. For everyone else, call reception and we'll help you arrange an appointment.

Good to know

Fees, rebates and referrals

Allied health professionals and visiting specialists provide their services independently of the practice, so their fees are separate from our GP fees. Please ask the provider or reception about fees, Medicare rebates and private health cover when you book.

To see Dr Kausar or our visiting clinical psychologist, you'll need a referral from your GP. If you have a chronic condition, your GP may also be able to arrange a care plan that gives you Medicare rebates for some allied health visits.
